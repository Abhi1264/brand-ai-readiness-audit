# Engagement checks

A new visitor should be able to answer:

1. Who is this?
2. What does this site offer?
3. Who is it for?
4. Why should I care?
5. What should I do next?

## Measurable homepage gaps

Report weak orientation when at least two of these are true:

- no H1
- no identity statement
- no CTA phrasing
- fewer than 20 visible words

## Continuation

Recommend only paths that exist on the site (do not invent a pricing page).

## Mobile

Only Playwright-observed metrics: `overflow_x`, `nav_visible`, `cta_visible`, `min_font_px`.
